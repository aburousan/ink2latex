About this project

ink2latex reads pen strokes directly. Photos are converted to strokes and are read less well than written ink. Recognition runs locally; your handwriting and photos stay on this device. After the first successful load, the demo works offline.

By Kazi Abu Rousan. Code: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 · Weights: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (credit required, no commercial use). Trained on MathWriting by Google Research (Gervais et al., 2024) and the author's own handwriting; see DATA.md.
